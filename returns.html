Returns & refunds

How we handle product concerns fairly and promptly.

Important: This is a launch-ready policy template. Review it against your actual operations and applicable Indian consumer and food regulations before launch.

Food product returns

For food-safety reasons, opened or used products generally cannot be returned because of a change of mind. Do not consume a product if its package appears damaged or tampered with.

Damaged or incorrect products

Contact us as soon as possible after delivery if you receive a damaged, leaking, expired or incorrect item. Share your order details and clear photographs of the outer parcel, label and product.

Resolution

After reviewing the concern, we may offer a replacement, store credit or refund as appropriate. Eligibility depends on the evidence provided and the condition of the product.

Refund timing

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method or another agreed method. Processing time depends on the payment provider.

Custom products

Personalised multigrain blends cannot normally be cancelled after preparation begins unless there is a quality or fulfilment issue.
