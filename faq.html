Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before placing your PoshanPantries order.

How do I place an order?

Add products and pack sizes to your cart, enter your delivery details and tap “Send order on WhatsApp”. We will confirm availability, shipping and payment with you.

How do I pay?

After your order is confirmed, we will send a secure payment link or UPI details on WhatsApp. The website does not collect payment directly.

Which pack sizes are available?

Pack sizes vary by product and generally range from 100 g to 1 kg. The available options are shown on each product page.

Can I create my own multigrain flour blend?

Yes. Choose the Custom Multigrain Flour product and tell us your preferred grains and quantity in the order notes. We will confirm the blend and final price.

Are the product photos real?

The current website uses clearly marked placeholder illustrations. These will be replaced with real product and packaging photographs before the final public launch.

How should I store the products?

Keep products in clean, airtight containers away from heat and moisture. Product-specific storage guidance is provided on each product page and pack.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery time depends on your PIN code and product availability. We will share the expected timeline before you make payment.

Can I change an order after sending it?

Yes, if preparation or dispatch has not started. Message us immediately on WhatsApp with the change you need.
