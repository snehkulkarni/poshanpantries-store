Privacy policy

Our plain-language approach to the information used for your order.

Information you provide

When you prepare an order, the site asks for your name, phone number, address and optional notes. This information is placed into a WhatsApp message only when you submit the form.

Cart storage

Your shopping cart is stored locally in your browser using localStorage so it remains available after a refresh. The cart is not sent to a server by this static website.

How information is used

Information sent through WhatsApp may be used to confirm products, calculate shipping, process payment, deliver your order and provide customer support.

Third-party services

WhatsApp, UPI providers, payment-link providers and courier partners process information under their own privacy terms. This site also loads Google Fonts when an internet connection is available.

Sharing and retention

Customer details should only be shared with service providers needed to fulfil an order and retained only as long as required for service, accounting and legal obligations.

Your choices

You may contact PoshanPantries to ask about your information or request a correction, subject to applicable legal and record-keeping requirements.
