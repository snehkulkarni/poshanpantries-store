# PoshanPantries — Project Brief

## Business and Target Customers

- PoshanPantries will be an online store for healthy, everyday food products.
- The main customers are women, families, and health-conscious buyers.
- The brand will focus on nutritious, convenient, and trusted kitchen essentials.

## Product Categories

### Seeds and Dry Fruits

- Pumpkin seeds
- Watermelon seeds
- Fenugreek seeds
- Cumin seeds
- Mixed seeds

### Spices

- Malvani masala
- Garam masala
- Turmeric

### Dehydrated Products

- Banana powder
- Carrot powder
- Tomato powder
- Onion powder
- Garlic powder

### Multigrain Flour

- Jowar + ragi + bajra
- Moong + jowar + ragi + bajra
- Wheat + jowar + bajra + ragi + maize
- Custom mixture based on the customer's choice

## Launch Range

- Approximately 20–50 products at launch
- Price range: ₹49–₹2,500
- Pack sizes: 100 g to 1 kg
- Products may have multiple weight options.

## Brand Identity

- Store name: **PoshanPantries**
- Brand personality: traditional Indian, clean, modern, and minimal
- Main colours: white, maroon, and fresh green
- Typography: clean, modern, and easy to read
- Design reference: [Organic Tattva](https://organictattva.com)

## Website Structure

- Home
- Shop / All Products
- Product category pages
- Product detail pages
- About Us
- Contact Us
- Cart
- Order confirmation
- FAQ
- Shipping Policy
- Returns and Refunds Policy
- Privacy Policy
- Terms and Conditions

A simple WhatsApp order-confirmation process will be used instead of a traditional online checkout.

## Product Images

- Real product photos are not currently available.
- Suitable dummy or placeholder product images will be used initially.
- These can be replaced with real product and packaging photos later.

## Orders and Payments

- Customers will select products, pack sizes, and quantities on the website.
- Orders will be confirmed through WhatsApp.
- A payment link will be shared with the customer through WhatsApp.
- An online payment gateway is not required for the initial launch.

## Customer Contact

- WhatsApp will be the primary contact and ordering channel.
- The website will include a floating WhatsApp button.
- A contact form will also be provided.
- The business phone number, WhatsApp number, and email address will be added when available.

## Recommended Features

- Offers and announcement banner
- Product search
- Category filters
- Pack-size and quantity selection
- Ingredients and product benefits
- Usage and storage instructions
- Nutrition information
- Shipping information
- Customer reviews
- Related products
- Frequently asked questions
- Custom multigrain flour enquiry
- WhatsApp ordering button
- Mobile-friendly design
- Basic search-engine and social-sharing setup
